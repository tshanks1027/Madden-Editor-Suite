What this does:

Scrapes complete team rosters from pro-football-reference.com for all 32 NFL teams (or fewer for historical years). Generates ~1,700 rostered players plus ~1,300 free agents to match official roster size (~3,000 total players). Converts season stats into Madden ratings.
